🔴 Common Suspicious Patterns in Crypto Transactions
1. Fan-In (Mixing / Aggregation)

What happens? Many different wallets → send funds → into a single address.

Why suspicious? Criminals try to combine dirty funds into one place before moving them.

Graph shape: 🔻 Funnel

 A ----\
 B ----- >  X
 C ----/


👉 Address X is suspicious (many inputs, one output).

2. Fan-Out (Distribution / Dusting)

What happens? One wallet → sends funds → to many small addresses.

Why suspicious? Laundering or distributing ransomware payments.

Graph shape: 🌳 Tree-like

     Y
   / | \
  A  B  C


👉 Address Y is suspicious (one input, many outputs).

3. Peeling Chain

What happens? Large wallet keeps sending small chunks out while moving the rest to itself (like peeling onions).

Why suspicious? Classic laundering method (step-by-step obfuscation).

Graph shape: 🔗 Chain

 X ---> Y ---> Z ---> W
  \-> small1
      \-> small2


👉 Each step peels off some coins to a fresh small address.

4. Looping (Cycling Funds)

What happens? Same funds move in circles between related addresses.

Why suspicious? Trying to confuse tracing.

Graph shape: 🔄 Cycle

 A --> B --> C
 ^           |
 |-----------|


👉 Red flag: transactions keep coming back.
